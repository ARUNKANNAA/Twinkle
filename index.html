1 Quick Start, Battery & Charging Guide

Twinkle is 100% portable and powered by an internal rechargeable battery. Follow these simple steps:

  1. Desk Placement & Portability: Place Twinkle anywhere on your workstation, monitor riser, bedside table, or carry it around. Because it is battery-powered, there are zero messy cables tethering it to your desk.
  2. Charging Twinkle (2 Hours): Connect any standard USB Type-C cable into the rear port. Charging for just 2 hours is enough for a full charge.
  3. Smart LED Charging Indicator: While plugged in, the rear indicator light glows RED to signify active charging. Once Twinkle is fully charged, the indicator light automatically turns BLUE!
  4. Slide Power Switch: Slide the rear tactile power switch to the ON position to power on.
  5. Instant Awakening: Twinkle's screen immediately illuminates with the TWINKLE startup logo followed by animated expressive eyes.
  6. Smart Sleep & Instant Wake: If untouched for 60 seconds, Twinkle enters a quiet battery-saving sleep state. A gentle tap on the top crown instantly awakens Twinkle with an eyelid flutter.

How Daily Morning Motivation Works

Twinkle isn't just about reminders; it's about starting your day with positive energy:

  • First-Wake Detection: Once Twinkle has synced its calendar date over Wi-Fi, it tracks the start of each new calendar day. When you wake Twinkle for the first time each morning, it automatically presents today's quote.
  • 30 Curated Offline Quotes: All 30 quotes are permanently stored inside Twinkle's microcontroller memory. No Wi-Fi or internet connection is required to read them.

4 Wi-Fi Setup & Precision Clock Sync

Twinkle connects directly to your home or office Wi-Fi with zero app store downloads:

  1. Hotspot Discovery: On first boot, Twinkle broadcasts a setup hotspot named Twinkle-Setup and displays an on-screen QR code.
  2. Connect Phone or Laptop: Scan the QR code or open Wi-Fi settings on your phone and select Twinkle-Setup.
  3. Web Portal: Your browser will automatically open the setup page. Select your Wi-Fi network, enter the password, and tap Save.
  4. Automatic Sync: Twinkle restarts, connects to your Wi-Fi, and automatically sets the digital clock and full calendar date within 3 seconds.
  5. Network Reset: To change networks at any time, hold the top sensor during boot to re-open the setup portal.

6 Troubleshooting & Official Support

Quick remedies for common questions:

  • Screen Not Turning On: Ensure the USB Type-C cable is firmly seated into the rear port and the rear slider switch is set to ON. Tap the top crown to check if Twinkle was asleep.
  • Time Not Updating: Confirm your Wi-Fi network has active internet access. Twinkle syncs automatically within 3 seconds of connection.
  • NFC Tap Not Triggering: Hold your smartphone near the front or crown of Twinkle. Ensure NFC is enabled in your phone settings.
  • Care & Cleaning: Wipe the pure white casing with a dry or lightly dampened microfiber cloth. Keep away from spilled liquids.
